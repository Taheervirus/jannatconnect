About us

Jannat Connect is a wireless internet business built on Starlink satellite technology. We make getting online simple: you pay, and we connect you.

Many communities still struggle with slow or unreliable internet. Starlink reaches places where cables and masts do not, and we are here to bring that service close to you, with local support from people you can visit or call.
